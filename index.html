AI that changes
what happens next.

A potential seven-hour build: learning tasks that adapt to the reason someone struggles, with explicit evaluation and uncertainty boundaries.

MISTAKELOOP ↗ CONCEPT

Same question.
Different misconception.
Different next step.

Instead of changing difficulty from a score alone, the proposed system would use the learner's reasoning to choose a targeted follow-up. Its AI output would remain subject to structured validation and review.

USERA learner who understands some parts, but gets stuck repeatedly.
AI ROLEInfer candidate misconceptions and propose a targeted follow-up.
SAFETY BOUNDARYUncertain evaluations do not silently award mastery.

This is an idea and a deterministic interaction illustration, not an existing AI agent or validated learning outcome.

04HOW I WORK

Not just a generated diff.

AI-assisted engineering still requires problem ownership, technical judgment, verification, and a clear account of what remains unknown.

01 / DEFINE

Start with the user's friction.

Describe what fails in the current workflow and which next action should become easier.

02 / DIRECT

Give Codex constraints.

Make requirements, architecture boundaries, and acceptance conditions explicit before implementation.

03 / REVIEW

Challenge what was produced.

Inspect the code, consider edge cases, and correct issues rather than treating generated output as proof.

04 / VERIFY

Show repeatable evidence.

Use tests and CI, distinguish simulations from deployment, and state the limits in writing.
